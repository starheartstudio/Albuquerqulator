Albuquerqulator

If you've ever wanted to know how many times you could listen to Albuquerque by Weird Al Yankovic while travelling to Albuquerque, then this is for you. Simply enter your starting location and pick your mode of transport. This space-aged application will calculate how many times the song will play with Swiss precision. Oh, you can also change the destination to something other than Albuquerque, but why would you want to go anywhere else?
